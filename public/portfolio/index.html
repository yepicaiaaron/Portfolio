Not a neat job title

I imagine it. I understand it. I build it. I bring people with me.

I am a creative technologist and storyteller who ships code. My work sits in the uncommon space between frontier AI research, product engineering, creative direction and human adoption.

I can read the paper, challenge the assumptions, work with the models and help build the system. I can also walk into a FTSE 100 leadership team, explain why it matters without hiding behind jargon, and give people the confidence to use it.

Then I can turn the same technology into something people want to experience: an AI confessional with Rory Sutherland, an interview coach for students, a national election avatar or a filmmaking movement that travels from London to Cannes and New York.

The rare part is not any one of those skills. It is carrying the idea all the way from possibility to public reality.

Computer vision led to generative video. Generative video led to building companies, products and experiences before the category had a name.
