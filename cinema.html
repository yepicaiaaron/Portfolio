06 / Abu Dhabi Aviation and Oracle

It all had to run
on their hardware.

Airport staff needed years of experience with short codes just to ask their systems a simple question. I led and coded an interface that let them ask in ordinary language, with charts and an avatar explaining the answer.

We couldn’t rely on cloud services. Speech recognition, the language model, voice and video all had to run locally, with different access permissions. Getting that responsive on an L40 was a proper engineering challenge.
