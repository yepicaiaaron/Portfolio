Reel 1 · A different kind of business

It started with a different way to do business

After volunteering in Cambodia, I wanted to help parents earn a living so their children could stay in education. At nineteen I started Fikay, making fashion accessories from recycled materials with local producers.

It taught me that a new idea only matters when you bring people into it.

British Empire Medal in 2016 for services to ethical fashion. University of Essex Alumnus of the Year in 2018.

Reel 2 · SeeFashion

What if the image was the question?

Twelve years ago I was working on visual search. With SeeFashion, I built around computer vision and machine learning, helping people find what they meant even when they didn't have the words.

Tap a piece in the window.

The camera reads colour, cut and shape, then looks for pieces that match. No typing needed.

Reel 3 · Yepic AI

One photo. A talking face.

I began working on AI video in 2019. At Yepic we made zero-shot talking photos: a single image could become a talking face, without training a new model for that person. We made it commercially available from 2020.

Reel 4 · Conversations, not clips

Not just a face. Someone to talk to.

We gave people a way to talk with an AI persona. My work joined the models, the interface and the live experience.

Reel 7 · Good Omen

Two products. Under twenty hours. First place.

With Giovanni Funchal and Marko Stapfner, our team Good Omen won the AI Engine Summer Hack with Summer Chat. Then at Project Europe × Lovable in Stockholm we built a journalist avatar and a real-time video tool in under twenty hours, and won.
