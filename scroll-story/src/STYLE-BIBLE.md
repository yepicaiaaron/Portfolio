# The Making Of - style bible

Story: Aaron Jones's career told as a film made in a paper cut-out picture house. The corner clock shows the year. The sky moves from dawn to day, night, dusk and a new dawn.

## Medium
- Layered paper cut-out diorama. Flat coloured card, crisp cut edges, soft paper drop shadows (`0 10px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.22)`).
- Paper fibre on large areas, fine halftone dots instead of gradients.
- Clean scissor cuts on objects and characters; torn, deckled edges on horizon bands.
- 4 to 6 planes: sky, far hills and treeline with a small town skyline, ground, midground props and characters, foreground grass strip.
- Flat soft light. Projector and spotlight beams are translucent pale cones (screen blend on the web).
- No text or logos in the art.

## Colour script (the year clock is the time of day)
- Opening and 2014-2015 (Fikay, SeeFashion): dawn, cream sky, low orange sun.
- 2019-2023 (Yepic, live experiences, enterprise): full day, cream to warm yellow sky.
- Interval (honours): foyer interior, velvet walls, cream noticeboard.
- 2024-2025 (Big Screen Hack, Good Omen): night, navy sky, projector beams, yellow lamps, confetti.
- Now (StoryMachine, world models): dusk, purple and navy torn bands, orange glow.
- Next (let's talk): a new dawn, the sun rising again.

## Motifs
Orange sun disc, the screen with velvet curtains, the film projector and its beam, red folding seats, film reels and strips, clapperboard, foreground grass strip, paper tape on panels, brass pins.

## Characters
- Lead: Aaron as a friendly big-head cartoon director (head about a third of the height), likeness from his portrait. One costume on every appearance (set from photos), plus a clapperboard or megaphone.
- Supporting cast: filmmakers (yellow scarf), engineers (teal overalls), audience (mixed, simple shapes).
- Every character: hero image, 5-pose sheet, one-page model sheet.

## Composition
- Keep the top half of each scene for action (phone panels sit at the bottom).
- Key action away from the panel side on desktop (panels alternate left and right).
- Horizon at 40 to 55% of the height; props share a floor line.
