01 · About

I make machines feel like people, without pretending they are.

Aaron Jones is a creative technologist and founder working at the intersection of AI, media and real-time systems.

For the last decade he’s led product and engineering at the edge of synthetic media — founding Yepic AI to build presenter-quality video from text, shipping SDKs used by Fortune-500 learning teams, and advising start-ups on how to make AI feel less uncanny and more inevitable. He was awarded a BEM in 2024 for services to innovation.

He keeps a small practice: one product at a time, one stage at a time, one long-form idea at a time. Research and experiments live in the open on GitHub.
